ACCA Exemption Eligibility (Track 2 Students)

If you have already completed BCom (Hons) or hold another recognised qualification, you may be exempt from some or all of BT, MA and FA. Exemptions are awarded directly by ACCA Global upon registration — this LMS does not grant exemptions itself.

StepWhat to Do
1Visit the official ACCA Exemptions Calculator and enter your qualification details.
2Note the indicative exemptions shown (BCom graduates commonly receive exemptions across some Knowledge and Skills papers, subject to your specific curriculum and university).
3Register with ACCA Global (or update your existing registration) to have exemptions formally confirmed and applied.
4Share your confirmed exemption letter with Dr. Shallu so your GJIMT LMS dashboard status is updated to "Exempt" for the relevant paper(s).
5Once exempt, you can skip directly to Applied Skills readiness materials (as they become available) rather than repeating Knowledge-level content — or opt to use this track purely as a refresher.

Real ACCA Exam Booking (When You're Ready)

BT, MA and FA are all on-demand Computer-Based Exams (CBE) — you can book and sit them at an approved exam centre any time, without waiting for a fixed session date. GJIMT's recommended readiness benchmark before booking: complete all 5 topics for that paper and score at least 60% on two consecutive Full-Length CBE-Replica Mock attempts (available on the Practice & Mock Tests page).

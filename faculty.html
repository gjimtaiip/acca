Exemption Processing Guide (Track 2 Students)

StepAction
1Collect the student's prior qualification documents (BCom Hons transcript, any existing ACCA registration/exemption letters).
2Direct student to the ACCA Global Exemptions Calculator to get an indicative entitlement.
3Confirm final exemption award only comes from ACCA Global directly upon official registration — this LMS calculator result is indicative only.
4For exempted papers, mark the student's dashboard status as "Exempt" and grant access to Applied Skills readiness materials (once built) instead of repeating Knowledge content.
5Exempt students who want a refresher may still opt into the Knowledge track voluntarily — track separately as "Refresher, not required for progression."

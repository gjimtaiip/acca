Exemption Processing Guide

StepAction
1Collect the student's prior qualification documents (BCom Hons transcript, any existing ACCA registration/exemption letters).
2Direct student to the ACCA Global Exemptions Calculator for an indicative entitlement.
3Confirm final exemption award only comes from ACCA Global directly upon official registration.
4Already-exempt students (e.g. Nyari) go directly to Applied Skills — no need to complete Knowledge-level modules.

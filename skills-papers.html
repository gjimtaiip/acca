LW — Corporate and Business Law

Format: Objective Test only (Section A: 45 Qs = 70 marks; Section B: 5 scenarios = 30 marks) · 2 hours · Pass mark 50%

Topic 1: Essential Elements of the Legal System

What this is about: Where law comes from, and how disputes get resolved without court.

Sources of law: Legislation (laws passed by parliament) and Case Law/Precedent (binding court decisions).

Court structure: Lower/Trial courts → Appeal courts → Supreme/Highest court (sets binding precedent for all).

ADR types: Arbitration = binding decision by neutral 3rd party (like a private judge). Mediation = facilitated negotiation, no binding outcome imposed.

Exam tip: Match the scenario to the right ADR method — arbitration for binding resolution, mediation for assisted negotiation.

Topic 2: Law of Obligations (Contract & Tort)

4 essentials of a valid contract: Offer, Acceptance, Consideration, Intention to create legal relations. Missing any = no contract.

Breach remedies: Damages (money — the main remedy), Specific performance (court orders performance), Rescission (contract cancelled, parties restored).

Negligence — 3 elements to prove: Duty of care existed → Duty was breached → Breach caused damage.

Exam tip: Professional negligence scenarios (e.g. bad accountant advice) — check all 3 negligence elements before concluding liability.

Topic 3: Employment Law

Employee vs self-employed: Tested by control, integration, mutuality of obligation. Employees get statutory protections; self-employed don't.

TypeMeaning
Wrongful dismissalBreach of CONTRACT (e.g. no notice given)
Unfair dismissalNo fair statutory REASON/procedure

Redundancy: The JOB no longer exists (not a performance issue).

Exam tip: Check for a valid statutory reason AND fair procedure to assess unfair dismissal.

Topic 4: Formation & Constitution of Business Organisations

Key distinction: Sole trader/partnership have NO separate legal identity (unlimited liability). A company has SEPARATE legal identity ("corporate veil") — shareholders get limited liability.

Incorporation: Register → file Articles of Association → receive Certificate of Incorporation → company is now a distinct legal person.

Exam tip: "Lifting the corporate veil" — courts can ignore limited liability in fraud cases.

Topic 2: Specialist Costing Techniques

Target Cost = Target Selling Price − Target Profit Margin

Life-cycle costing: ALL costs across a product's life (design → disposal), not just manufacturing.

Throughput Accounting: Rank products by throughput per unit of the BOTTLENECK resource.

TQM 4 quality costs: Prevention, Appraisal, Internal failure, External failure.

Exam tip: Throughput questions — always identify the bottleneck first.

Topic 3: Decision-Making Techniques

Relevant costing rule: Only future, incremental cash flows. Ignore sunk costs.

Breakeven units = Fixed Costs / Contribution per unit

Limiting factor: ONE scarce resource → rank by contribution per unit of THAT resource. TWO+ scarce resources → use Linear Programming.

Exam tip: The classic trap is ranking by contribution per unit instead of per unit of scarce resource.

Beyond Budgeting: Critiques rigid annual budgets for encouraging short-termism/gaming.

Mix & Yield variances (splits of usage variance for multiple materials): Mix = different proportion used; Yield = output achieved from inputs.

Exam tip: PM wants written interpretation of WHY variances arose, not just the number.

Topic 5: Performance Measurement & Control

Transfer pricing: Price charged between divisions of the same company. If external market price exists, that's usually the fairest basis.

Building Blocks model (Fitzgerald & Moon): Dimensions, Standards (fair, achievable, owned), Rewards.

Exam tip: Check for an external market price first before recommending cost-based transfer pricing.

Topic 2: Income Tax Liabilities (Individuals)

Employment Income + Other Income − Personal Allowance = Taxable Income → apply rate bands

Progressive bands: You never pay the top rate on ALL income, only the portion in that band.

Exam tip: Follow the standard computation order exactly — "own figure" credit is given even if an earlier number is wrong.

Topic 3: Corporation Tax Liabilities

Accounting Profit +/− Adjustments − Capital Allowances = Taxable Total Profits

Capital Allowances = tax's own version of depreciation, completely separate rates from accounting depreciation.

Exam tip: Always start from the accounting profit figure given, then adjust line-by-line.

Topic 4: Chargeable Gains

Sale Proceeds − Allowable Costs = Chargeable Gain − Reliefs = Taxable Gain

Rollover relief: Defers gain when reinvesting in a new qualifying business asset.

Exam tip: Always check for available reliefs BEFORE calculating final tax.

Topic 5: Inheritance Tax & Other Taxes

IHT: Charged on estate value above the nil-rate band, plus certain lifetime gifts.

Taper relief: Reduces IHT rate on gifts as more years pass before death.

Exam tip: Get the "look-back" timeline right before calculating IHT on lifetime gifts.

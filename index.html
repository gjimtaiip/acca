Knowledge Level — 3 Papers (Parallel Tracks)

BT Business & Technology

Business organisation, governance, external environment, leadership, communication, ethics. Objective-test only exam.

MA Management Accounting

Costing, budgeting, standard costing & variances, performance measurement. Section A (OT) + Section B (3 MTQs).

FA Financial Accounting

Double-entry bookkeeping, trial balance, financial statements, basic consolidation, interpretation. Heaviest-weighted paper.

⚡ Already completed BCom (Hons) or hold other qualifications?

You may be eligible for ACCA exemptions on some or all of BT, MA and FA (BCom graduates typically receive up to 5 Knowledge + Skills-level exemptions from ACCA Global, subject to individual assessment). Exempt students should skip directly to Applied Skills readiness modules once available, or use this Knowledge track purely as a refresher before Skills level. Check your exact exemption entitlement on the Resource & Exemption Guide page.

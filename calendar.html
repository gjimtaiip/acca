How the Parallel-Track Calendar Works

Each paper has its own faculty and moves at its own pace, but all three share the same weekly rhythm: study the topic → attempt the Topic Practice Test (instant scoring + wrong-answer explanations) → sit that paper's Weekly Mock on Saturday. Once every topic in a paper is covered, students move into unlimited-attempt Full-Length CBE-Replica mocks pulled randomly from the question bank, until ready to book the real ACCA exam.
